Presentation summary

This presentation introduces the Smart Wallet Rewards Optimizer, a recommendation-only agent that combines merchant and wallet tools, issuer-document retrieval, deterministic Tree-of-Thought evaluation, and selective human review. The web app offers local Qwen and optional Anthropic Claude models for bounded language tasks, and displays its ToT evaluation and audit trail with each result. The presentation summarizes the architecture and its development, reports automated test results while distinguishing them from a real-world benchmark, and discusses limitations and next steps.
